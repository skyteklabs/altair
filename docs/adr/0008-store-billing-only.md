# Premium is sold only through the App Store and Play Store

Subscriptions are bought only through in-app purchase on iOS and Android; Palmy runs no direct payments. Palmy's own Promo codes grant free Premium periods rather than discounting a price. We chose this over adding an Indonesian payment gateway (Midtrans/Xendit) because the stores largely require in-app purchase for digital subscriptions anyway, and direct billing would bring tax, invoicing and refund handling.
