# Staff never see a Space's financial data

Palmy staff using the internal dashboard can manage people's logins, Subscriptions and Promo codes, but cannot see or edit anything inside a Space: no Wallets, Transactions, Budgets or Holdings. Finance data is the most sensitive thing people give Palmy, and "support can read your money" is a trust and compliance risk (including under Indonesia's personal data protection law) that outweighs the convenience for customer service.

## Consequences

- The dashboard has no read access to Ledger data. Aggregate, anonymised metrics are the only exception.
- If support access is ever added, it must be an explicit, time-limited grant made by the person, not a staff permission. That would be a new ADR.
